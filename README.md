# Chih-Hsuan (Bella) Yang

I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Argonne National Laboratory** working on **LLM agents, multi-agent systems, and agentic evaluation**. I build research harnesses for matched protocol comparisons and study reviewer-to-solver critique uptake, answer repair, task-conditioned memory, and inference quality-cost trade-offs.

My prior industry work includes training preference models from experimental outcomes and adapting language models with **DPO and PPO** at Corteva, with scientist preferences elicited when the model was uncertain. At Google X, I studied information flow, coordination, and bidding among stakeholder agents. These experiences connect agent research and preference learning with **AI for Science**.

## Methods and implementation

- **Agentic evaluation:** protocol orchestration, structured traces, controlled memory ablations, and critique/repair diagnostics.
- **Efficient reasoning:** supervised protocol routing, held-out tests, confidence calibration, and token/call accounting.
- **Preference-based post-training:** experiment-grounded rewards, scientist feedback, preference modeling, DPO, and PPO.
- **Implementation:** Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, NumPy/pandas, Hugging Face, pytest, and Git; reproducible Linux/HPC experiments.

## Research experience and mentoring

- **Argonne National Laboratory:** agent evaluation harnesses, matched protocol studies, trajectory-level failure analysis, cost-aware routing, and controlled memory evaluations. I participate in the AuroraGPT Post-Training Working Group and mentor four students.
- **Corteva Agriscience:** preference models grounded in experimental outcomes; language-model adaptation with Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), informed by scientists' preferences when model judgments were uncertain.
- **Google X:** multi-agent LLM frameworks representing stakeholders with different interests, studying information flow, coordination, communication, and bidding.

Earlier work includes materials and molecular discovery and scientific machine learning. See my [CV](https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/cv/) for appointments and education, and my [publication list](https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/publications/) for the full research record.
