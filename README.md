# Chih-Hsuan (Bella) Yang

I am a **Postdoctoral Researcher at Argonne National Laboratory**. I build research harnesses for large language model (LLM) agents and study how collaboration, feedback, and memory affect reasoning reliability and inference cost. My work connects **multi-agent systems**, **evaluation**, and **post-training** with **AI for Science**.

## Research experience and mentoring

- **Argonne National Laboratory:** research on agent evaluation, trace analysis, feedback, memory, and cost-aware inference. I mentor four students and coordinate collaborative research through experiments, manuscripts, and reproducible releases.
- **Corteva Agriscience:** preference models grounded in experimental outcomes; language-model adaptation with Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) and Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO), informed by scientists' preferences when model judgments were uncertain.
- **Google X:** multi-agent LLM frameworks representing stakeholders with different interests, studying information flow, coordination, communication, and bidding.

Earlier work includes materials and molecular discovery and scientific machine learning. See my [CV](https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/cv/) for appointments and education, and my [publication list](https://chihhsuan-yang.github.io/publications/) for the full research record.
